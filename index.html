About us

Building character, one adventure at a time

We are the T.M.M.V (N.S) Boy Scouts, the scout troop of Km/Tk/Thambiluvil M.M.V. (National School), part of the Sri Lanka Scout Association, Akkaraipattu Kalmunai District. Our scouts learn practical skills, lead their patrols and serve their community.

🧭

Leadership

Patrol leaders plan activities and run meetings, learning to lead by doing.

🏕️

Outdoor life

Day hikes, camp-outs and trainings that teach teamwork and resilience.

🤝

Service

Clean-up drives, tree planting and helping hands wherever the village needs them.
